Welcome to Phone Cleaner: Storage & Junk Cleaner ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by DevGen Ai Inc. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, what permissions we require, how on-device data is processed, and our commitment to transparency.

Key Privacy Guarantee:

All file scanning, junk cleaning, duplicate file finding, and image compression activities take place 100% locally on your device. We do not transfer, upload, or store your private files, photos, videos, or documents to any external server.

1. Information We Collect and Process

We believe in absolute data minimization. Our application does not require user accounts, registration, or collection of personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, physical address, email address, phone number, or contacts.

A. Device Information Processed Locally:

  • Storage & File Data: Scans temporary cache files, obsolete APKs, empty folders, and duplicate files to calculate storage savings and facilitate user-approved deletions.
  • Installed Applications: Reads installed application names, package names, and app sizes to power the built-in App Manager and memory cleaner.
  • Hardware & Battery Statistics: Reads battery level, charging status, temperature, RAM usage, and CPU core details strictly to display real-time device health to you.

2. Android Permissions We Request & Why

To deliver core device management and optimization features, our application requests the following Android permissions in full adherence to Google Play Developer Policies:

All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Required on Android 11 (API 30) and above for core device cleaning capabilities: scanning junk cache across system directories, identifying duplicate files across storage partitions, and deleting user-selected files.

Read & Write External Storage (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Required on devices running Android 10 and older to enable file scanning, junk removal, duplicate file identification, and saving compressed images.

Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

Required for the App Manager and RAM Booster features to inspect installed apps, display their storage footprint, and allow users to uninstall or manage unwanted apps.

Kill Background Processes (KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES)

Used exclusively by the RAM Booster feature to terminate idle background tasks and reclaim free system memory upon explicit user action.

Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Required solely for network connectivity to serve advertisements (Google AdMob), deliver remote app configurations (Firebase Remote Config), and record anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).

4. Data Retention and Security

Because file analysis and cleaning operations are executed locally on your device hardware, we do not maintain external databases containing your files or device state. When you delete files or uninstall apps using our tools, those actions are performed directly on your local Android file system and are irreversible.

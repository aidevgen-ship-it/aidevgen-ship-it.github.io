Privacy Policy

App Name: Contacts & Dialer • Last Updated: October 2026

Summary: Contacts & Dialer processes your contacts and call logs exclusively on your device to provide dialer, caller ID, and call management functionality. We do not collect, upload, share, or sell your personal contacts, phone numbers, or call logs to any external server.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Contacts & Dialer ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our Android mobile application.

By using Contacts & Dialer, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Permissions and Data We Access

To operate as a full-featured default phone dialer and address book, the app requests access to the following sensitive permissions:

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS): Needed to display your address book, search contacts, add/edit/delete contacts, and display caller names during incoming and outgoing calls. All contact data stays strictly on your physical device.
  • Call Logs (READ_CALL_LOG, WRITE_CALL_LOG): Required to show your call history (incoming, outgoing, and missed calls) in the Recents tab, allow you to clear call entries, and redial recent contacts.
  • Phone & Calling (CALL_PHONE, READ_PHONE_STATE, MANAGE_OWN_CALLS): Essential to make phone calls directly from keypad/contacts, detect incoming calls, manage in-call states (mute, speaker, hold), and function as the Default Phone Handler.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely when you tap the voice search icon to find contacts hands-free. Audio is processed transiently and is never recorded or stored.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to alert you of incoming calls, ongoing call status, and missed call notifications.

3. Data Usage & On-Device Processing

We believe strongly in user privacy:

  • No Cloud Upload of Personal Data: Your phonebook, contact details, and call history are never uploaded to any remote server or database operated by us.
  • No Selling of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information, phone numbers, or calling history to data brokers or third parties.
  • App Vault & Private Storage: Private contacts locked inside the App Vault are stored locally on your device in secure app storage protected by your chosen PIN.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app may display advertisements and use analytics to support ongoing maintenance. These third-party providers may collect non-personal diagnostic and advertising data:

  • Google AdMob: Uses device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and app usage metrics to serve banner and interstitial ads according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Used to deliver non-personal configuration settings (such as ad frequencies and feature toggles).

5. Data Retention & User Control

All contact and call log data resides on your device's native SQLite storage managed by the Android operating system. You retain complete control:

  • You can view, edit, or delete contacts and call records at any time directly in the app.
  • You can revoke permissions at any time via Android System Settings > Apps > Contacts & Dialer > Permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately removes all app-specific caches, settings, and vault data.

6. Children's Privacy

Contacts & Dialer is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

7. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, and we take commercially reasonable measures to protect it. Since all core data remains stored strictly on your device, it is secured by your device's operating system security and encryption.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Smart Dialer & Phone Contacts processes your address book, contacts, and call history exclusively on your physical device. DevGen AI Inc does not collect, transmit, store on cloud servers, or share your personal contacts or call logs with any third parties.

1. Introduction & Developer Identity

This Privacy Policy is issued by DevGen AI Inc ("we", "us", or "our"), the official developer and legal entity responsible for the Android mobile application Smart Dialer & Phone Contacts (Package ID: com.phone.contacts.dialer.call), published on Google Play.

We respect your privacy and provide this policy to explain our on-device data processing, permissions required for telephony and contact management, and third-party advertising disclosures.

2. Sensitive Permissions & Data Access

To operate as a Default Phone Handler and contact manager, Smart Dialer & Phone Contacts requests access to the following Android runtime permissions:

A. Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS)

Required to display your address book, search contacts, add/edit/delete contacts, and identify callers during incoming and outgoing calls. All contact data stays strictly on your physical device and is never uploaded to external servers.

B. Call Logs (READ_CALL_LOG, WRITE_CALL_LOG)

Required to show your incoming, outgoing, and missed call history in the Recents tab and allow you to manage call records. Stored strictly in your device's native system call log.

C. Phone & Calling (CALL_PHONE, READ_PHONE_STATE, MANAGE_OWN_CALLS)

Essential to place phone calls directly from keypad and contacts, detect incoming phone calls, control in-call states (speaker, mute, hold), and serve as the Default Dialer on Android.

D. Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used strictly when you tap the voice search icon to search contacts hands-free. Audio is processed transiently on-device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

E. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used to provide incoming call heads-up notifications, ongoing call notifications, and missed call alerts.

3. Data Usage & On-Device Privacy Architecture

  • Zero Cloud Upload of Personal Data: Your contacts, phone numbers, and call logs NEVER leave your physical device. DevGen AI Inc does not maintain servers that store your address book or call logs.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal contacts, phone numbers, or calling history to data brokers or third parties.
  • Local Vault: Contacts placed in the App Vault are encrypted and stored solely in secure app storage on your device, protected by your custom PIN.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app integrates reputable third-party SDKs to support app development and maintenance:

  • Google AdMob: Serves ads. Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers such as Google Advertising ID and device diagnostic information to deliver relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. AdMob does not have access to your personal contacts or call logs.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Delivers non-personal configuration values, ad display rules, and performance updates.

5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Rights

Because your personal data resides on your device:

  • Direct Control: You can view, modify, or delete contacts and call records at any time within the application.
  • Permission Revocation: You can revoke any permission granted to the app at any time via Android System Settings > Apps > Smart Dialer & Phone Contacts > Permissions.
  • Instant Data Removal: Uninstalling the application completely erases all local caches, settings, and Vault contacts from your device.
  • Deletion Requests: Since DevGen AI Inc does not collect or store your personal communications data on remote servers, no server-side retention exists. For any data or privacy inquiries, contact us directly at aidevgen@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Smart Dialer & Phone Contacts is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We value your trust. All core app operations run locally on your device and benefit from the Android operating system's security architecture, sandbox isolation, and device encryption.

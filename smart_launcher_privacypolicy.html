3. Sensitive Permissions & Core Launcher Functionality

To provide an authentic launcher and lock screen replacement experience, the app requires specific Android device permissions. Below is the full disclosure of each permission and its exact purpose:

Important Disclosure regarding QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES:
Smart OS Launcher & LockScreen is a default Home Launcher app (declared with android.intent.category.HOME). In order to display your installed applications on the Home Screen, dock, App Library, and quick search bar, the app requires access to query the installed apps on your device. This information is processed strictly on-device in real-time. We never store, log, or transmit your installed application list to any external server or third party.

Detailed Permissions Overview:

  • Query All Packages (android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Used strictly to list and launch your installed applications, organize apps into smart categories in the App Library, and provide search indexing within the launcher.
  • Flashlight / Camera Flash (android.permission.FLASHLIGHT & android.hardware.camera.flash): Used exclusively to toggle the quick flashlight tool on the Lock Screen and Control Center. The app does NOT capture, record, store, or transmit any photos or video.
  • Storage / Gallery Access (android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below, or System Photo Picker): Used solely when you choose to set a custom wallpaper from your personal photo gallery. Your photos remain private on your device; they are never uploaded to the cloud.
  • Show When Locked (showWhenLocked="true"): Used to allow the custom iOS-styled Lock Screen to appear when you wake your device display.
  • Network & Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to display advertisements via Google AdMob and to check network availability.
  • Input Method Service (android.permission.BIND_INPUT_METHOD - Optional): If you activate the optional Smart OS Keyboard, keystrokes are processed strictly on-device to type into active text fields. No keystroke logging, password tracking, or remote transmission ever occurs.

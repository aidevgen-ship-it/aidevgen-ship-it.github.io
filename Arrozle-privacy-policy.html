DevGen Ai Inc. ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Arrozle: Arrow Puzzle Game (hereinafter referred to as the "Service"). This Privacy Policy outlines our guidelines regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download and play our game.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a continuous and smooth gaming experience, Arrozle stores essential game-related data locally on your device:

  • Level completion status and progress
  • Puzzle board states and unlocked shapes
  • Lives, hints, sound settings, and user preferences

This information is saved locally via device storage. We do not store or transmit this personal gameplay data to any external private server. Clearing your device app data or uninstalling the game may reset this stored progress.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Our game integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect anonymous diagnostic and technical device information:

A. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads)

We use Google AdMob to serve ads (interstitial and rewarded ads). Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address and coarse location
  • App interactions, ad impressions, and crash diagnostics

This data is used to provide, personalize, and measure advertisements, detect fraud, and maintain service reliability. For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

B. Google Firebase Remote Config

We utilize Firebase Remote Config to dynamically update game configurations and optimize user experience without requiring full application updates. Firebase may process installation IDs and technical telemetry to deliver configuration parameters securely. Read more at Firebase Privacy and Security.

3. Children's Privacy

Arrozle: Arrow Puzzle Game is designed for a general audience, including families and children. We comply with child privacy laws, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us at aidevgen@gmail.com, and we will take prompt steps to delete such data.

4. Data Retention and Security

Local game data remains on your device until manually cleared or uninstalled. Third-party partners retain technical log data according to their retention policies and utilize industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to secure data in transit.

5. Your Choices & Opt-Outs

  • Reset / Delete Advertising ID: You can manage, reset, or delete your Advertising Identifier anytime through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete Advertising ID.
  • Erase Local Data: You can clear all locally stored data via: Settings > Apps > Arrozle > Storage > Clear Storage.

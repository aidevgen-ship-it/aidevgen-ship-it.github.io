This policy explains what information FitHabit handles and the choices you have.

Information saved on your device

FitHabit uses an on-device profile. You can use the app without creating an online FitHabit account.

  • Profile and fitness information. This may include the name you enter, gender, age, height, weight, target weight, fitness goal, experience level, push-up ability, focus areas, and weekly workout target. FitHabit uses these details to personalize your workout experience and calculate estimates such as BMI.
  • Workout history and preferences. FitHabit saves session dates, duration, completed exercises, estimated calories, saved exercises, customized sets and reps, and app preferences on your device. It uses this information to show your activity, remember your plan, and track your goals and streak.
  • Reminders. If you enable workout reminders, FitHabit schedules local notifications at your chosen time. You can turn reminders off in the app or revoke notification permission in your device settings.

FitHabit does not intentionally send your on-device fitness profile or workout journal to its advertising or analytics services. Depending on your settings, your operating system may include app data in device backups or transfer it to a new device.

Information processed by online services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase. FitHabit uses analytics to understand app use and improve the experience. Google may process app instance or device identifiers, device and app information, screen views, session information, and other app interactions.
  • Firebase Crashlytics. FitHabit uses Crashlytics in release builds to diagnose errors and improve stability. Reports may include crash stack traces, relevant app and device information, installation identifiers, and recent app interactions associated with an error.
  • Firebase Remote Config. FitHabit fetches configuration values, including ad settings. Firebase may use an installation identifier and related technical information to serve the appropriate configuration.
  • Google AdMob and consent tools. When advertising is enabled, FitHabit may show interstitial, rewarded, or app-open ads. Google may process IP addresses, advertising or other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, manage privacy choices, and prevent fraud. An IP address can indicate a general location. Ad personalization depends on your choices, device settings, and applicable rules.
  • Connectivity checks and app updates. FitHabit checks internet access using requests to Google endpoints. These requests expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address. Google Play also handles supported in-app update checks and installation.

Google and its service providers handle this information for the functions described above. Advertising data may also be shared with advertising partners as part of ad delivery and measurement. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy, and Firebase privacy and security information.

Your choices

You can update your profile inside FitHabit. Where Google requires an in-app consent control, use Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change your choices. Your operating system and Google account may offer additional advertising controls, including My Ad Center.

Retention, deletion, and security

FitHabit keeps your profile, workout history, and preferences on your device while they are needed to provide the app features. On Android, clear FitHabit’s app storage in device settings to remove its local profile and workout history. Uninstalling the app removes its local copy, although an operating-system backup may still exist and could restore data.

Clearing app storage does not delete records already received by online services. Retention of analytics, crash reports, and ad data depends on the service and its configured retention settings. Contact us with a privacy or deletion request and we will explain what information we can identify and what options are available.

FitHabit stores its local records in app-private storage. Online requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when FitHabit’s features or data practices change. The revision date at the top identifies the current version.

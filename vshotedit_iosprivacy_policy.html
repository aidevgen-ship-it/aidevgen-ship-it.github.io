Privacy Policy

V-Shot Pro - Video Editor

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Welcome to V-Shot Pro ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Ketan Pipaliya / CS Code Tech. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data that may be processed when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, local processing, device permissions, and third-party advertising in accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. 100% Local On-Device Video Processing

Your media privacy is our utmost priority. All video editing tasks—including cutting, trimming, merging clips, adjusting playback speed, rendering text overlays, auto captions, audio mixing, and photo collage creation—are performed 100% locally on your device using client-side processing libraries.

No Cloud Uploads: We do not upload, copy, store, transmit, or analyze your personal photos, videos, or audio recordings on any remote server or cloud infrastructure. Your creations never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share them via third-party apps.

2. Device Permissions We Request

To provide full video and photo editing functionality, V-Shot Pro requests access to certain device hardware and storage features with your explicit consent:

  • Photo Library / Storage Access (iOS: NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription & NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Required exclusively so you can select photos and videos from your camera roll to edit within the app, and to save exported videos and photo collages directly to your Photos library.
  • Camera Access (iOS: NSCameraUsageDescription): Used only when you choose to capture new photos or video clips directly from within the app for editing.
  • Microphone Access (iOS: NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Used only when you choose to record a voiceover commentary or audio clip to accompany your video project.
  • Network Access: Required to load remote app configuration (via Firebase Remote Config) and to serve advertisements (via Google AdMob).

You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's System Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

V-Shot Pro is supported by advertisements. We integrate verified third-party SDKs that may collect pseudonymous technical data strictly for ad delivery, analytics, and app reliability:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): AdMob displays banner and interstitial advertisements in the app. Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS with user permission, or GAID on Android), IP addresses, diagnostic logs, and ad interaction metrics to deliver relevant ads and combat fraud. For further details on Google's privacy practices, please review the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Remote Config & Analytics): Used for dynamic app configuration and general app stability without collecting personal media.

4. Data Retention and User Accounts

V-Shot Pro does not require user registration, logins, or user accounts. Because we do not transmit or store your personal media on external servers, we hold no personal data to retain or delete. You retain complete ownership and full control over all media files residing in your device's local storage.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

V-Shot Pro is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take steps to remove such information.

6. Security

Because all media processing is handled locally within the operating system's sandboxed environment, your photos, videos, and projects are protected by your device's built-in operating system security protocols.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will become effective immediately upon posting the updated policy on this page or within the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

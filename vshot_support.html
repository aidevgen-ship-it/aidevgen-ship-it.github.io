Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new video project?

Open V-SHOT PRO and select Video under Create New. Choose your media, arrange clips on the timeline, then add text, music, transitions, and effects as needed.

How do I generate AI Auto Captions?

Open AI Auto Captions, choose the speech language, and start transcription. Review and edit the generated captions before exporting your video. Availability may depend on your app version and selected language.

How can I add music to my video?

Open a video project, tap Music, select an available track or supported audio source, and adjust its position and volume on the timeline.

How do I export and save my edited video?

Tap Export in the video editor, select an available resolution and frame rate, and wait for processing to finish. You can then access the saved video from your gallery or the app's creations section, depending on device permissions.

How do I restore my premium purchase?

If you purchased a subscription or in-app product, look for Restore Purchases in the app's premium or settings section, if available. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If you need assistance, email support with your purchase details; never send passwords.

What should I do if the app crashes or export fails?

Restart the app, check available device storage, update to the latest version, and try exporting again. If the issue continues, contact support with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the problem.

How can I share feedback or report a bug?

Email support@kmsteams.com with a brief description and, if possible, a screenshot showing the issue. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.
